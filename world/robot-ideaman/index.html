What is The Idea Dealer?

The Idea Dealer is a simple project idea generator for moments when you have coder's block. Coder's block is like writer's block: you want to make something, but your brain cannot pick a project to start with.

When you press a key or click the button, The Idea Dealer chooses a random coding idea from a list and shows it on the page. It is meant to help you stop staring at a blank screen and quickly find something fun to build, practice, or remix into your own project.
