No-Screen The Idea Dealer Build
========================

Goal:
Press a physical button on the robot and hear The Idea Dealer say a random project idea.

Hardware:
- Arduino Uno
- Push button
- USB cable
- Laptop or computer nearby
- Speaker
- Cardboard or wood robot body

Wiring:
- One side of the button goes to Arduino pin 2.
- The other side of the button goes to Arduino GND.

How it works:
1. The button is pressed.
2. The Arduino sends the word "idea" over USB Serial.
3. robot_ideaman.py hears the message.
4. The computer chooses an idea.
5. The speaker says the idea out loud.

Good physical layout:
- Put the button on the chest.
- Hide the speaker in the head or chest.
- Run the Arduino USB cable out the back to the laptop/computer.
- The robot does not need a monitor.

Best command:
cd C:\Users\sguls\world\robot-ideaman
python .\robot_ideaman.py
