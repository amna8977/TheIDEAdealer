IDEA man Robot Build Setup Ideas
================================

Recommended robot height:
- 4 feet 11 inches can work well.
- This makes the robot feel more human-sized and like a real character.

Suggested proportions:
- Total height: 59 inches
- Head: 12 to 16 inches tall
- Torso: 24 to 30 inches tall
- Base/legs: 14 to 20 inches tall
- Width: 20 to 28 inches
- Button height: 36 to 44 inches from the floor

Screen placement:
- Put the screen in the chest or face area.
- Chest placement is easier if you are using a laptop, monitor, or tablet.
- Put the button just below the screen so people can press it and see the idea.

Bottom/base:
- Use a heavy, wide base so the robot does not tip over.
- A good base size is about 24 to 30 inches wide and 16 to 24 inches deep.


Things to put inside the base for weight:
- Books
- Bricks
- Sandbags
- Water bottles
- Dumbbells

Design idea:
- Make the base look like a Vegas stage platform.
- Use black, red, and gold colors.
- Add card suit symbols.
- Add fake casino lights.
- Put an IDEA man sign on the front.

Important tip:
- Avoid skinny legs as the main support.
- Build the real support as a sturdy box or platform.
- You can add cardboard legs on the outside just for decoration.
